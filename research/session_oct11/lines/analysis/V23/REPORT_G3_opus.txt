  under the address key every 89885b75 launch is a singleton because each launch calls its own launcher contract). 11 families, 1,924 pools. o02, o02b.
- Replay (v3sim.py): the pool = its positions from Mint/Burn; the price before our buy = the last Swap's recorded sqrtPriceX96; before any swap,
  the token-side edge of the first mint (no Initialize row in the census; a buy crosses the empty range for free, so it trades identically);
  our buy of q at the head of block A = anchor + K; every later swap re-applied by its input amount with Uniswap V3's SwapMath step
  arithmetic (fee per step, SqrtPriceMath rounding, tick crossing between ranges); value recorded at the end of every block A..A+199 plus the
  in-block maximum (take-profit trigger). Anchor = the first-mint block (= the launch block for every family except 13ead562, whose mint
  comes 5-9 blocks after the createAndInitializePoolIfNecessary). 18 K (K = 0 an upper bound, never selected) x 143 exits (11 holds; 8
  take-profits x 11 deadlines; 4 next-buy sizes x 11 deadlines) = 2,574 rules per family. A Burn with liquidity > 0 at or before the sell
  block = -100%; no trade if the pull is at or before A - 2. Gas $0.02 per round trip is inside every grid return (C's convention puts it
  only in $; the calibration below uses C's).
- Our wallet 0xab5a34c3 / relay 0x2abb5653: in no V3 Swap (sender or recipient), Mint/Burn owner, or fetched tx (o03, o04); the code removes
  them anyway.
- Rule choice: V = fit days 1-5 mean > 0 (n >= 10), validation days 6-7 mean > 0 (n >= 5), every 6-hour slice with >= 3 trades positive,
  highest validated $/day (validation $ / 2); F = best fit total among rules with every fit slice positive, measured on days 6-7; W = V's
  choice on days 1-6, measured on day 7; A = best 7-day $ among the rules V accepts. o08.

LIMITS
- Who bought is known only for fetched transactions (first 12 swaps, first real buy, pulls, up to 12 buys before a pull): 21,514 of 139,705
  V3 swap txs; inside the V windows 22% (crowd line) and 44% (89885b75 356 B) of the buy ETH has no fetched tx. Wallet pool counts are lower
  bounds (19,593 wallets; equal to the census's own count for 1,530 of 1,574 V3 first-real-buy wallets).
- Min-out is readable only for SwapRouter02 exactInputSingle; the crowd's bot contracts (Universal Router execute) are unreadable, so the
  replay cannot show whether our buy ahead makes their buys revert; reverted transactions are not in the data.
- V3 Initialize is not in the census: the start price is the mint's token-side edge (exact for buys from a token-only position; 50 two-sided
  mints in lc:0a493d1a|ac9650d8 use sqrt(lower) + amount1/L).
- Landing at +2 (and +1 for 13ead562's K1) was measured on v4 only. The 13ead562 mint tx and its selector are not cached.
- The 356-B family has 111 launches, 83 of them in the last 48 h; its slice test rests on 9 slices.
- V3 pools outside factory 0x1f7d7550, and non-WETH V3 quotes, are not in the census.
